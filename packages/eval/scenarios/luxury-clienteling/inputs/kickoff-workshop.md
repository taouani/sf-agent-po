# Clienteling programme: kick-off workshop

Date: 4 November 2026. Attendees: Claire Martin (Retail Director), Hugo Lambert (CRM Product Manager), Mei Chen (Head of Boutiques, Asia), Tom Reed (IT Architect), two client advisors from the Paris flagship.

## Context

Maison Aurelle sells watches and high jewellery through 14 boutiques (Paris, London, Milan, Shanghai, Tokyo, Dubai and others) and its website. Client advisors keep their client book in notebooks, Excel and WhatsApp. Sales Cloud is used today only by the wholesale team. About 180,000 clients; 2,500 of them are VIC (top tier).

## What advisors need

- One client profile on their phone: identity, preferences (sizes, metals, stones, styles), important dates, family and assistant contacts, purchase history from all boutiques and the website.
- Their own client book, with reminders before birthdays and anniversaries, and a weekly list of clients to contact.
- Wishlists and "pieces on hold" for a client.
- Book boutique appointments, also from the website.
- See after-sales repairs and their status (watch servicing takes 6 to 10 weeks at the Geneva workshop).
- Invite clients to launches and private sales, and record who came.

## Decisions and figures

- Purchase history comes from the POS (Cegid) and the e-commerce platform. Cegid stays the system of record for sales and stock.
- Client tiers (Essential, Gold, VIC) are computed from 24-month spend. Marketing wants the tier to be visible to advisors but not the exact spend amount.
- Clients often buy in several countries. An advisor in Paris must be able to see a client who also buys in Dubai, but only their own boutique's advisors may edit the client's notes.
- Boutique connectivity is poor in two locations (Dubai Mall basement, Milan): the app must work offline for viewing the client book.
- Go-live wanted for Paris and London before the spring launch on 15 March 2027; Shanghai and Tokyo in the second wave.

## Success measures

- 70% of boutique sales linked to an identified client within 6 months (today 35%).
- Every VIC contacted at least once a quarter.
