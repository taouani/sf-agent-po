## Salesforce products to consider

Commerce Cloud (B2C and B2B), Consumer Goods Cloud for retail execution, Loyalty Management, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud) for a unified customer profile, Order Management.

## Data model

Person Accounts or contacts for shoppers, orders and order items, loyalty programs and members, store visits and audits for field reps, products and price books.

## Regulation and compliance

PCI DSS for payments, GDPR and CCPA, consent and preference management, consumer protection.

## Typical integrations

ERP and order management, POS, e-commerce platforms, payment providers, logistics and returns, product information management.

## Watch for

Peak-season volumes, identity resolution across channels, and keeping transactional history in Data 360 or the data platform rather than CRM records.
