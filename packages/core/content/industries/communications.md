## Salesforce products to consider

Communications Cloud (Enterprise Product Catalog, Industries CPQ, Industries Order Management), Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, Media Cloud for advertising sales.

## Data model

Billing and service accounts, product catalog with offers and bundles, quotes, orders and assets for installed services.

## Regulation and compliance

Telecom regulator rules, privacy of usage data, lawful-intercept and data-retention obligations handled in network systems.

## Typical integrations

BSS/OSS stacks, billing, provisioning and network inventory, often through TM Forum Open APIs.

## Watch for

Catalog complexity and order decomposition, very large asset volumes, and clear ownership between CRM and BSS.
