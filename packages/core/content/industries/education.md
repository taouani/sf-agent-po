## Salesforce products to consider

Education Cloud (recruitment and admissions, student success, advancement) or the older Education Data Architecture, Experience Cloud for student portals, Marketing Cloud.

## Data model

Learners and contacts, academic programs and terms, applications, enrolments, advising and success plans, alumni and advancement.

## Regulation and compliance

FERPA in the US, GDPR, safeguarding for minors, accessibility.

## Typical integrations

Student information systems (for example Ellucian Banner or Colleague, Tribal SITS, Workday Student), learning management systems, identity providers.

## Watch for

The SIS is usually the system of record for academic data; agree data ownership. Confirm whether the org is on Education Cloud or EDA.
