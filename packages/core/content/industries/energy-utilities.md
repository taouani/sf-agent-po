## Salesforce products to consider

Energy and Utilities Cloud, Service Cloud, Field Service, Experience Cloud for customer self-service, OmniStudio.

## Data model

Premises, service points and meters, service accounts, programmes and enrolments (for example energy efficiency), field work orders.

## Regulation and compliance

Regulator rules on customer protection and vulnerable customers, critical infrastructure security, privacy of consumption data.

## Typical integrations

Customer information and billing systems (for example SAP IS-U), meter data management, outage management, GIS.

## Watch for

Billing and meter data stay in the CIS and MDM; design for read-through or summaries. Very large customer bases mean data volume and query selectivity matter.
