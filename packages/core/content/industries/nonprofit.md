## Salesforce products to consider

Nonprofit Cloud (fundraising, programs and case management, grantmaking, volunteer management) or the older Nonprofit Success Pack, Experience Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud).

## Data model

Donors as Person Accounts and households, gift commitments and gift transactions, campaigns, programs, enrolments and benefits, volunteers. NPSP uses a different, package-based model: confirm which one the org runs before designing.

## Regulation and compliance

GDPR and consent, fundraising regulation, tax relief rules (for example Gift Aid in the UK), PCI DSS for online donations.

## Typical integrations

Payment processors and donation platforms, finance systems, email marketing, wealth screening.

## Watch for

Migration between NPSP and Nonprofit Cloud is a redesign, not an upgrade. Small admin teams favour configuration over code.
