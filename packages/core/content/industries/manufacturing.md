## Salesforce products to consider

Manufacturing Cloud (sales agreements, account-based forecasting, rebates, warranty), Sales and Service Cloud, Field Service, Experience Cloud for partner and distributor portals, Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud) for connected products.

## Data model

Sales agreements and their schedules, account forecasts, rebate programs, assets and warranty terms, partner accounts. ERP remains the system of record for products, prices and orders.

## Regulation and compliance

Export control and sanctions screening, product safety and recall traceability, GDPR for contacts, industry quality standards (for example ISO 9001) for service processes.

## Typical integrations

ERP (SAP, Oracle) for products, pricing, orders and invoices, PLM, IoT platforms for connected assets, distributor and channel data feeds.

## Watch for

Product and price master data ownership, forecast volumes and recalculation jobs, partner data visibility, and large asset and service history volumes.
