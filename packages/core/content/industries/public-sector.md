## Salesforce products to consider

Public Sector Solutions (licensing and permitting, inspections, benefits and case management, grants), Experience Cloud for citizen portals, OmniStudio, Service Cloud.

## Data model

Individuals and business accounts, applications, licences and permits, inspections, cases, benefits and grants.

## Regulation and compliance

Government security frameworks (for example FedRAMP with Government Cloud Plus in the US, or national equivalents), accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA, Section 508), data residency, records management and freedom-of-information obligations.

## Typical integrations

Identity providers (for example Login.gov or national digital identity), payment gateways, legacy case and finance systems, GIS.

## Watch for

Hosting and residency constraints may limit which features and AI services can be used; confirm before designing. Citizen portals need accessibility and high-volume guest or authenticated access patterns.
