## Salesforce products to consider

Financial Services Cloud with its insurance data model, OmniStudio for quoting and claims intake, Experience Cloud for policyholder and agent/broker portals, Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud).

## Data model

Insurance policies with participants, coverages and assets, claims, producers (agents and brokers), quotes. Confirm the insurance objects licensed in the org; policy administration often stays in an external system of record.

## Regulation and compliance

GDPR or local privacy law, Solvency II (EU) or state insurance regulation (US, NAIC), IFRS 17 reporting (finance systems, not Salesforce), fair-treatment and complaints-handling rules, record retention.

## Typical integrations

Policy administration and billing systems (for example Guidewire, Duck Creek, Sapiens), rating engines, claims systems, document generation, payment providers.

## Watch for

Which system owns the policy and the premium, renewal and endorsement processes, broker and agent access in the portal, consent for marketing, and claims data sensitivity (health data in life and health insurance).
