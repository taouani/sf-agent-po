## Salesforce products to consider

Automotive Cloud, Sales and Service Cloud, Experience Cloud for dealer portals, Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud) for vehicle and driver data, Marketing Cloud.

## Data model

Vehicles and vehicle definitions, ownership and driver relationships, dealers as partner accounts, leads and test drives, service and warranty history.

## Regulation and compliance

Privacy for connected-vehicle and driver data (GDPR, CCPA), consumer finance rules for captive finance, recall obligations.

## Typical integrations

Dealer management systems, OEM order and allocation systems, telematics, captive finance platforms.

## Watch for

Dealer versus OEM data ownership and sharing, volumes of vehicle telemetry (keep it in Data 360 or off-platform), recall campaign scale.
