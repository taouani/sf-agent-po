## Vague words

Words that make an acceptance criterion or a non-functional requirement impossible to test. A
word ending in `*` also matches the words it starts ("encrypt*" matches "encrypted"). "Quick" is
not listed: Quick Actions are a Salesforce feature.

- fast
- user-friendly
- user friendly
- easy
- easily
- intuitive
- appropriate
- appropriately
- etc
- and so on
- as needed
- seamless
- seamlessly
- robust

## Generic personas

Personas that name nobody in particular, with or without an article.

- user
- users
- someone
- customer
- person

## Articles

- a
- an
- the

## Compliance terms

Words that mark an input with security or compliance requirements, which a requirement or a
story must then cite.

- security
- secure
- encrypt*
- gdpr
- hipaa
- pci
- sox
- mfa
- sso
- privacy
- personal data
- pii
- pipl
- compliance
- audit*
- retention
- consent

## Joining words

Words that often join two needs in one statement.

- and also
- as well as
- and then
- in addition
