# Business banking onboarding: discovery workshop notes

Participants: Head of Business Banking, onboarding operations lead, relationship managers (2), compliance analyst.

- Onboarding a small business client takes 21 days on average; target is 5 days.
- Relationship managers (RMs) collect documents by email and re-key data into the core banking system.
- KYC checks are run manually in a separate screening tool; results are pasted into a spreadsheet.
- Clients want to upload documents and see their onboarding status online.
- Each business can have several directors and beneficial owners; each needs identity verification.
- RMs should see every onboarding case for their clients; operations sees all cases in their region only.
- The bank already uses Salesforce Sales Cloud for RMs. Financial Services Cloud licences were purchased last quarter but are not configured.
- Accounts are opened in the core banking system after approval; Salesforce must receive the account number back.
