# PI 2027.1 planning: committed objectives (Customer Digital ART)

| # | Objective | Business value | Committed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Policyholders can view and accept their home and motor renewal quote online | 10 | Yes |
| 2 | Renewals agents get a 30-day renewal worklist in Salesforce | 8 | Yes |
| 3 | Online payment of the first instalment at renewal | 7 | Yes |
| 4 | Mid-term adjustments online | 5 | No (stretch) |

Dependencies
- PAS team to expose a renewal quote API (REST) by sprint 3 of the PI.
- Payments team to provide a hosted payment page integration.

Risks
- PAS API performance under peak renewal load (about 4,000 renewals per day in December).
